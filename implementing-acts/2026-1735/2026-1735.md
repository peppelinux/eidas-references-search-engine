<div id="tit_1" class="eli-main-title">

COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING REGULATION (EU) 2026/1735

of 15 July 2026

amending Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1569 as regards applicable standards and specifications

</div>

<div id="pbl_1" class="eli-subdivision">

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION,

<div id="cit_1" class="eli-subdivision">

Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union,

</div>

<div id="cit_2" class="eli-subdivision">

Having regard to Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 July 2014 on electronic identification and trust services for electronic transactions in the internal market and repealing Directive 1999/93/EC <a href="#ntr1-L_202601735EN.000101-E0001" id="ntc1-L_202601735EN.000101-E0001">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">1</span>)</a>, and in particular Articles 45d(5), 45e(2), 45f(6) and 45f(7) thereof,

</div>

Whereas:

<div id="rct_1" class="eli-subdivision">

|  |  |
|----|----|
| \(1\) | To ensure the highest level of harmonisation among Member States for the development of European Digital Identity Wallets, the technical specifications for the wallets rely on the work carried out on the basis of Commission Recommendation (EU) 2021/946 <a href="#ntr2-L_202601735EN.000101-E0002" id="ntc2-L_202601735EN.000101-E0002">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">2</span>)</a> and in particular the architecture and reference framework. As the architecture and reference framework has evolved significantly since the adoption of Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1569 <a href="#ntr3-L_202601735EN.000101-E0003" id="ntc3-L_202601735EN.000101-E0003">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">3</span>)</a>, Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1569 should now be amended accordingly to align it with the new standards and specifications. |

</div>

<div id="rct_2" class="eli-subdivision">

|  |  |
|----|----|
| \(2\) | In accordance with the objectives of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, a number of standards have been selected to meet the requirements applicable to the issuance, management and verification of electronic attestations of attributes. These standards should reflect established practices and be widely recognised within the relevant sectors. Where necessary, these standards should be adapted or complemented in order to ensure a high level of security and integrity of electronic attestations of attributes, while facilitating cross-border interoperability and the effective functioning of the internal market. In line with this objective, this Regulation updates the requirements applicable to the issuance and revocation of qualified electronic attestations of attributes and electronic attestations of attributes issued by or on behalf of a public sector body responsible for an authentic source and introduces the requirements for the signing and sealing of verification results against authentic sources. |

</div>

<div id="rct_3" class="eli-subdivision">

|  |  |
|----|----|
| \(3\) | Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council <a href="#ntr4-L_202601735EN.000101-E0004" id="ntc4-L_202601735EN.000101-E0004">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">4</span>)</a> and, where relevant, Directive 2002/58/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council <a href="#ntr5-L_202601735EN.000101-E0005" id="ntc5-L_202601735EN.000101-E0005">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">5</span>)</a> apply to all personal data processing activities under this Regulation. |

</div>

<div id="rct_4" class="eli-subdivision">

|  |  |
|----|----|
| \(4\) | The European Data Protection Supervisor was consulted in accordance with Article 42(1) of Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 of the European Parliament and of the Council <a href="#ntr6-L_202601735EN.000101-E0006" id="ntc6-L_202601735EN.000101-E0006">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">6</span>)</a> and delivered its opinion on 17 April 2026 <a href="#ntr7-L_202601735EN.000101-E0007" id="ntc7-L_202601735EN.000101-E0007">(<span class="oj-super oj-note-tag">7</span>)</a>. |

</div>

<div id="rct_5" class="eli-subdivision">

|  |  |
|----|----|
| \(5\) | The measures provided for in this Regulation are in accordance with the opinion of the committee established by Article 48 of Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, |

</div>

HAS ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

</div>
